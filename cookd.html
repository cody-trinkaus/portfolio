Section 01Case Brief

The team spent 20 weeks working in HCDE 302 and 303 to design a mobile app that helps college students share and discover affordable recipes. As the UX Designer, I focused on user research, wireframing, and prototyping to build an intuitive and accessible experience that meets students’ needs.

Section 02Competitive Analysis

I conducted a competitive analysis of Factor to gain an understanding of the health food preparation and delivery industry and how our app would fit into and compete with it.

Section 12Establishing Branding

Typography

For Cook’d’s typeface, we chose Quicksand and Inter. Quicksand brings a friendly, approachable vibe, making the app feel personal and inviting. Inter ensures readability with its clean, modern design, perfect for easy navigation. Together, they create a smooth, user-friendly experience.

Mood-board

Our mood board features clean, warm-toned, and friendly photos to create an inviting feel. The final design uses different shades of green to reflect freshness and simplicity.

Color

I chose a green color palette with dark and light accents to give the app a fresh and elegant vibe.

Section 13Industry Review

Again, we presented our final prototype and demo video and engaged in conversation with industry professionals who provided feedback on our designs, concept, and process. They provided us with valuable insight into the strengths and weaknesses of our prototype.

Section 14What I learned…

My biggest point of learning in this process was the concept of identifying human needs within a problem space and then using those to determine the best solution to the problem. The research tools, such as interviews, market analysis, and concept testing are key skills that I developed during this project. If I were to start this project again with what I know now, I’m not sure that an app would be my result.

What could be better?

More user testing early on would have revealed ways to make the high-fidelity design cleaner and ensure pages feel cohesive. Future iterations should focus on enhancing visual consistency and polish.

Future opportunities…

We’d love to explore a physical prototype that helps users utilize the app while having their hands full, whether that’s through voice control or motion detection, to create more seamless and accessible experiences.
