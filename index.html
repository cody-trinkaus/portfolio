Field notes

I work between brand and product. I like problems with a lot of ground to cover: many users, many surfaces, a long history nobody wrote down.

My habit is to survey first. I talk to the people on the ground, map what already exists, and only then draw the route. The result is usually less new than expected and much easier to walk.
